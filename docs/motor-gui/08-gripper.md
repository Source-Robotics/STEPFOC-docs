# Gripper

A driver set up as a gripper (`#Gripper 1`, saved) shows the gripper controls: calibrate the jaws, a position from
open to closed, a speed and a current limit, and the state the gripper reports — holding an object, at the
position, the gripper's errors. *No gripper connection* means the driver is not in gripper mode or not on the bus.
What gripper mode is: [Gripper mode](../06-operation/gripper-mode.md).
