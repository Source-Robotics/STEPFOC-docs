# Hardware revisions

What changed in each revision of the STEPFOC board, and which firmware and which revision of this manual go with
it. The board reports its hardware revision in `#Info` (`Hardware version`) and in Send_Respond_Device_Info.

| Revision | Since | Changes | Firmware | Manual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 | first production | — | 101 and newer; 105 and newer ship with the bootloader | this manual |

Boards shipped with firmware 103 or older have no bootloader; [Installing the bootloader on an older board](../07-firmware/bootloader.md)
brings them to the current firmware.
